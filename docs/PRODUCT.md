# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A undergraduate student at Católica, on macOS, reading long HTML topics in the
Brightspace portal. The side panel stays open next to the text for 20 to 60
minute sessions, almost always at night. The main mode is **following along on
screen**: they read with the voice, using the highlighted passage as a guide.
Part of the time they only listen while doing something else.

Job to be done: finish the material without reading fatigue, without losing the
place and without starting over, and leave with the essentials retained.

## Product Purpose

Turn a portal topic into a reliable read-aloud session: correct pronunciation,
position preserved, a pause at figures, and a spoken summary at the end. The
panel exists to control that reading, not to compete with the text. Success is
the student forgetting the panel exists until they need it.

## Brand Personality

A nighttime study companion, warm and dry. A capybara professor lives in the
panel. She does not throw a party, does not chase streaks and does not speak for
the student. She reacts to what happens (reads, waits, thinks, sleeps) like a
deskmate who respects silence. The humor is observational, never slapstick.

A green banker's lamp, a stack of books and a dark forest room set the mood:
calm, low-fi, made for a long evening of study. The instrument language is shared
with Waypoint Labs: Geom and a chartreuse signal for what is live; the lamp's
half-light marks a pause.

The product voice is that of a study monitor: short sentences, direct verbs,
zero manufactured enthusiasm. Trust comes from precision (position, time,
state), not adjectives.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate cuteness**: stock mascot, shiny eyes, emoji, confetti, baby talk.
  The capybara is a real animal, drawn with restraint.
- **Stationery craft**: cream plus display serif plus terracotta, the bullet
  journal look. Any warmth here comes from the lamp, not from paper texture.
- **AI SaaS**: gradients, glassmorphism, floating cards, a hero with a big
  metric.
- **Consumer player** (Spotify, YouTube Music): oversized artwork, saturated
  color, controls as spectacle.
- **Gamer green**: neon lime, RGB glow, black plus acid green.
- **Institutional portal**: Brightspace blue, back-office density.

## Design Principles

1. **The text belongs to the portal; the panel is the hand on the wheel.** The
   material and its title are context, not the hero. The hero is where the
   reading is now.
2. **The mascot is the state, not the decoration.** Each mood answers a real
   app state. If it communicates nothing, it is not there.
3. **The light says what is happening.** The lamp halo, the watch ring and the
   status dot tell the same story before any word.
4. **Frequent control at hand, rare adjustment behind one step.** Play,
   previous, next and speed stay visible; voice and figure pause stay folded.
5. **Visual silence is confidence.** No ornament that does not carry state. A
   color has a license to appear only when it means something.
6. **The panel must never look broken.** Formatted numbers, legible error
   states, a disabled button that explains itself, no orphan ellipsis.
7. **The instrument and the room share one language.** Geom and a chartreuse
   signal for live state come from Waypoint Labs; the lamp's half-light marks a
   pause. The forest, the lamp and the capybara keep the room warm. A color earns
   its place by meaning something.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal requirement, with comfort as the floor: body text at least 12 px,
contrast at least 4.5:1 (15.9:1 for primary text, 11.1:1 for secondary),
touch targets at least 36 px, visible focus on every control, motion disabled
under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and a layout that holds from 320 px wide. No
state relies on color alone; there is always text or shape.
